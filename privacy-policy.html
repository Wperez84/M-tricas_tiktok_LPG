Grupo LPG — Metricas_LPG

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Metricas_LPG ("the Application") collects, uses, and handles information when you authorize it to access data from your Facebook Pages, Instagram Business accounts, and TikTok accounts.

2. About the Application

Metricas_LPG is an internal tool developed by Hashtag Agencia Digital for Grupo LPG (La Prensa Gráfica and El Gráfico). Its sole purpose is to collect publicly available performance metrics — such as views, likes, comments, and shares — from content published on Facebook Pages and Instagram Business accounts managed by Grupo LPG.

The data is used exclusively to generate internal performance reports for advertising clients of Grupo LPG media properties.

3. Data We Access

The Application requests access to the following data through the Meta Graph API:

  • Page performance metrics (views, reach, likes, comments, shares) via pages_read_engagement and read_insights
  • List of pages managed by the authorizing user via pages_show_list
  • Published content from pages via pages_read_user_content
  • Instagram Business account metrics via instagram_basic and instagram_manage_insights

For TikTok, when the account owner authorizes the Application through TikTok Login Kit, we access basic profile information (user.info.basic) and the list of the account's own public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts (video.list) through the TikTok Display API. We do not post, modify or delete content, and we do not access private messages.

4. How We Use Your Data

Data accessed through this application is used exclusively to:

  • Populate internal performance dashboards and reports
  • Track advertising campaign results for Grupo LPG clients
  • Generate periodic metrics summaries stored in Google Sheets

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties. Data is never used for advertising targeting, profiling, or any purpose beyond internal performance reporting.

5. Data Storage

Performance metrics retrieved through the API are stored in private Google Sheets accessible only to authorized Grupo LPG staff. No personal user data is stored — only aggregated public metrics (counts of views, likes, comments, shares) associated with specific posts or pages.

6. Data Retention

Metrics data is retained for reporting purposes for up to 24 months. Access tokens are stored securely within the Google Apps Script environment and are not exposed externally.

7. User Rights

The authorization granted to this application can be revoked at any time through your Facebook account settings under "Apps and Websites" or, for TikTok, under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services. Revoking access will immediately prevent the application from retrieving any further data.

8. Third-Party Services

This application uses the Meta Graph API. Use of that API is governed by Meta's own Platform Terms and Privacy Policy. The Application uses the TikTok Display API under TikTok's Privacy Policy. It also uses the YouTube Data API v3, governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
